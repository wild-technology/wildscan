## Key observations

1. **Calibration sidecars at align time massively reduce
   fragmentation**: fresh zone_1 gave 3 components at 97.0% vs
   production zone_1's 9 components at 96.7% (batched pre-sidecar).
   Same imagery, same box.
2. **The bow landed inside zone_1** this time (density cut differs from
   production's 2-zone split) and aligned as its own component (c2,
   bbox E 594653–668 / N 2345217–251) — the multi-feature intent
   realized by the solver itself, no merge needed.
3. **zone_2 (852 images, 11.9% registration) is transit/water-column
   imagery** between the wreck and the west pocket. Its 91 orphan
   contribution is genuinely unregistrable content, production zone_2's
   "48 orphans" pattern at scale. Its one component was a full twin of
   zone_3's and was dropped with zero information loss.
4. **Hull fusion costs 2 cameras and was auto-rejected.** All three
   ladder rungs fused c0+c1 into a 3,738-camera component — 2 short of
   the 3,740 input sum. Exact-additivity attribution flagged it,
   the never-shrink + exact-membership acceptance rejected adoption,
   and the intact originals carried into the assembly. OWNER DECISION
   at the gate: accept hull as two overlapping components (current
   state, zero loss), redo the fuse interactively in the GUI, or add a
   bounded-loss acceptance mode (≤ N cameras) to the driver.
